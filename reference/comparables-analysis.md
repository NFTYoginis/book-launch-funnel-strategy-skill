# Comparables analysis — honest, and honest about its own sourcing

Studying real competitor/comparable books and launches for structure, not for hype to copy. Grounded in the real trail's own comparables work — which was itself degraded by a blocked fetch tool, making it a real example of the failure mode to avoid, not a methodology to imitate.

## What comparables analysis is actually for

Not to find a template to copy — to find the **honest positioning gap**. Studying how comparable books/launches are structured (channel mix, funnel-vs-companion-site choice, pricing if any, launch-sequence shape) surfaces what's already crowded and what's genuinely open. The real trail's own instinct on a comparable launch machine: name what makes the scale mechanism work (synchronized cohort timing, a sold artifact, a recurring community layer), then find the specific axis — often screening, honesty, or depth — where this book's positioning has room to differentiate rather than compete head-on.

## Separate sourced from reconstructed, every time

The real trail's own comparables research needed to establish specific claims about a comparable creator's audience and business model, but a primary-source fetch tool was blocked for the entire job. Rather than presenting search-digest summaries as if they were confirmed facts, the file that resulted opened with an explicit sourcing-confidence banner and tagged individual findings with their actual confidence level — the same discipline as `fact-claim-verification-skill`'s UNVERIFIED banner (see that skill's `reference/unverified-sourcing.md` for the full mechanics). Apply it here identically: if a comparable's numbers, pricing, or claims come from a summary rather than the primary source, say so before presenting the finding, and mark it as needing verification before it goes into a launch strategy document as a settled fact.

## What a comparables pass should actually produce

1. **Structure, not hype.** How is the comparable's launch or funnel actually built — channel mix, sequencing, the funnel-vs-companion-site choice, monetization mechanism if any? Structure is copyable-as-a-pattern; specific hyped claims are not something to imitate.
2. **The honest positioning gap.** Given what's already crowded, what's the specific claim or stance this book can make that a comparable can't or won't? The real trail's own example: a category dominated by outcome-promise marketing has real room for a book whose positioning is "the honest one" — a debunk-friendly stance that a hype-driven comparable structurally can't adopt.
3. **A named confidence level per finding**, not a single blanket confidence for the whole comparables set. Some facts about a comparable are independently verifiable (a URL exists, a program is publicly listed); others are only as solid as a search-digest summary. Keep them visibly distinct.

## Failure mode to name, not repeat

The real trail's comparables research needed to answer a specific, central question and couldn't reach the primary sources to answer it directly. The failure mode worth avoiding on the next book isn't "sometimes a fetch tool is blocked" — that's going to happen. The failure mode is **not disclosing it**, or letting a positioning strategy quietly rest on unconfirmed digest summaries treated as settled fact. Per `rules.md`'s Research needed guidance: a live comparables scan runs per book, at use-time — if it fails or degrades, that gets named in the output, not silently worked around.

## Worked reference — what the real disclosure looked like

When a primary-source fetch tool was blocked for an entire comparables research job, the resulting analysis opened with a banner naming exactly what was blocked, stated that every downstream finding was reconstructed from secondary summaries rather than primary sources, and closed with an explicit instruction that specific figures needed verification before use in the book or in a marketing claim. Individual findings inside the file carried their own confidence tags rather than inheriting one blanket assessment. That's the standard to hold a new comparables pass to, whenever live research access is anything less than fully clean.
