# Email-sequence structure

A 6-email structure, value-first, CTA arriving only at the end. Read directly from `GabeYoga-HQ/Detox Book/launch/email-sequence.md`, a real, complete 6-email launch sequence.

## The structure

| # | Beat | Job |
| - | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Welcome** | The core question/promise, stated plainly. Sets the honest tone immediately — no softening-up for a later pitch. Names what's coming, and names who this isn't for (a real safety/exclusion note, not boilerplate). |
| 2 | **Setup** | Practical preparation for the thing being taught or offered — "the night before" in the real trail. Concrete, low-pressure, written to work whether the reader acts this week or just reads along. |
| 3 | **How-to** | The actual walkthrough — "the morning of" in the real trail: what to expect, stage by stage, told straight, fear-reduction through specificity ("here's exactly what this is really like") rather than hype. |
| 4 | **Objection-handling** | Addresses the hardest, most honest moment — where a reader is most likely to feel like giving up or that it isn't working — and reframes it as exactly the useful part, not a failure state. Includes the "it's okay to stop" permission explicitly. |
| 5 | **Return / re-entry** | The part almost every launch sequence skips: what happens *after* the core experience, handled as carefully as the experience itself. The real trail's own insight: "how you come back matters more than how you [did the thing]." |
| 6 | **CTA to purchase** | Ties the whole sequence back to the book's actual thesis, states the book's honest limitations plainly (what it won't promise), and makes the ask — the first and only purchase CTA in the sequence. |

## Value-first, CTA-second — the operator's own standing rule

Every email before the last one teaches or gives something real and usable on its own, independent of whether the reader ever buys anything. The ask doesn't appear until it's earned by five emails of actual value. A sequence that fronts the ask, or repeats it every email "just in case," breaks this rule and reads as exactly the softening-up the real trail's own Email 1 explicitly promises not to do.

## Subject-line variants

Each email in the real trail pairs its beat with a subject line that earns the open honestly — no clickbait, no manufactured urgency. Multiple subject-line variants can be tested per email, but every variant has to pass the same test: does it tell the truth about what's inside, in a way that creates real curiosity rather than a false promise the email then has to walk back.

## Threading the book's own protected lines through for consistency

The real trail reuses the book's own signature language across the sequence rather than inventing separate marketing copy — the same central question from the book's jacket line opens Email 1 and gets explicitly paid off in Email 6; a phrase load-bearing enough to be "protected" elsewhere in the book's own material (see `book-to-content-repurposing-skill`'s seam-legibility and asset-inventory disciplines) shows up in the emails verbatim, not paraphrased into different marketing-voice wording. This keeps the launch sequence reading as an extension of the book's actual voice, not a separate sales-voice bolted on top of it.

## The standing footer

A consistent disclaimer/safety footer runs on every email in a sequence dealing with any physical practice — the same discipline as `fact-claim-verification-skill`'s standing micro-disclaimer. It doesn't get abbreviated away just because it's "email 4 of 6 and everyone's seen it already."

## Worked reference — the real sequence's shape

Email 1 (welcome) states the book's whole honesty stance up front and names real exclusions (who shouldn't try the practice right now). Emails 2–3 (setup/how-to) walk through practical preparation and a stage-by-stage expectation-setting narrative that defuses fear through specificity. Email 4 (objection-handling) meets the reader exactly at the hardest moment and reframes stopping early as success, not failure — carrying the book's own "'not now' is not failure, it's information" line verbatim. Email 5 (return) covers the come-back phase in as much detail as the main event, naming the real risk of undoing the value of what came before. Email 6 ties back to the book's actual deeper thesis, states three honest limitations plainly, and makes the single purchase ask of the whole sequence — using the locked-token pattern (`reference/locked-token-launch-pattern.md`) for the buy link itself.
